Post-production

Video post-production

In post-production a film gets its rhythm, colour and sound — everything viewers perceive as quality. We take advertising and branded content from raw footage to masters for broadcast and digital.

How we work in post-production

Post-production starts before the shoot. In pre-production we agree on running times, versions, graphics and the packshot so that everything needed for the edit is shot on set.

The footage then goes through editing, colour grading, sound and graphics. At each stage the client reviews an interim version and gives notes, and the number of revision rounds is agreed at the start.

We do post-production for our own shoots and bring footage shot by other teams to final quality.

What's included

Editing

Building the story and rhythm, rough and fine cut, versions of different lengths.

Colour grading

The film's visual style, consistent colour between shots, accurate reproduction of product and packaging colours.

Sound

Sound design, voice-over recording, music, mixing and loudness to broadcaster and platform specs.

Graphics

Motion graphics, titles, packshot.

Mastering and delivery

Broadcast masters, files for digital platforms, vertical versions, subtitles.

What it solves

  • Bring footage up to broadcast quality.
  • Turn one film into a set of versions for TV, YouTube and social media, including vertical formats.
  • Prepare Kazakh and Russian versions.
  • Adapt a foreign commercial for the Kazakhstan market: voice-over, titles, packshot.
  • Update an existing film: new prices, a promotion, a shorter cut.

Who it's for

  • Brands and agencies with footage already shot that need the final edit.
  • Agencies adapting global campaigns.
  • Firmachi clients as part of full-cycle production.

Why Firmachi

  • Post-production works hand in hand with directing, so the visual approach holds all the way to the master.
  • We know broadcast and platform specs and prepare versions for them from the start.
  • One producer manages the project, the schedule and revisions.

FAQ

Can I order post-production only?+
Yes. Send a description of the footage and the task, and we'll estimate the scope and timing.
How should footage be handed over?+
Camera originals, sound and editing notes if you have them. We agree on the transfer format before work starts.
Will you make versions for different platforms?+
Yes. A broadcast master, plus versions for YouTube and social media in the right aspect ratios and lengths. The list of versions is fixed at the start.
Do you make Kazakh and Russian versions?+
Yes, with voice-over recorded in both languages and adapted titles and graphics.

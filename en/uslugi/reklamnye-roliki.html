Advertising film production

A commercial works when the idea, the image and the production all serve one brand goal. Firmachi runs the project from brief to master: directing, producing, shooting and post-production in one team.

How we make commercials

We start with the campaign goal: what the viewer should understand and feel, and on which media the film will run. This determines the running time, the dramaturgy, the framing and the number of versions.

Then the director prepares a treatment: the visual approach of the future film — references, light, colour, casting, locations, edit rhythm. In parallel the producer builds the budget and the production plan. The client and the agency see what the film will look like and what it will cost before preparation begins.

All key decisions are locked at the pre-production meeting (PPM): actors, locations, costumes, props, storyboard, shift schedule. No questions that could have been settled in advance are left for the set. Shoot time goes into the image and the performances.

What production includes

Development

01

Brief analysis, creative concept or adaptation of the agency's script, director's treatment, budget and schedule.

Pre-production

02

Editing, colour grading, sound, graphics and packshot, masters for broadcast and digital. More on the post-production page.

FAQ

How much does a commercial cost to produce?+
The budget depends on running time, number of shoot days, locations, casting and the complexity of post-production. We prepare a budget after the brief and show what it is made of.
How long does production take?+
On average about 14 days from approved script to finished master. If the film is needed by a specific date, the plan works back from it: for example, the team organised the shoot for the Exeed model launch film in a week.
Do you work from an agency's script?+
Yes. The agency owns the creative, and we take on directing and production. The director's treatment is approved with the agency and client before preparation starts.
What will we receive?+
A broadcast master to TV channel specs, versions of different lengths, and versions in the right aspect ratios for digital platforms. The list of versions is agreed at the start so it can be planned into the shoot.
